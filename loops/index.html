LE-VAI

Loops, on repeat.

Each loop ends exactly where it begins. Rendering the last instant gives a frame identical to the first, and the encodes add no more artifact at the seam than anywhere else in the file. They play while on screen. Every loop has its own pause button, and none of them start on their own if you have asked your system for reduced motion.

Rendered with HyperFrames. Proof tooling and sources: LE-VAI/LE-VAI.
